YOUR MONTHLY WORKFLOW (2026)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  1. DOWNLOADS — Automatic
     Bank and credit card activity download throughout the month.
     You do not need to do anything for this step.

  2. CHECK CATEGORIES — When you have a few minutes
     Toolbar: Check Categories
     Confirm the category looks right for each charge. Change it if not.
     Use "Always for this vendor" when the same merchant should always go
     to the same place.

  3. RECONCILE — When the monthly statement is available
     Toolbar: Reconcile
     Match your books to the bank statement. If something is missing or
     miscategorized, fix it here (Fix category, add missing items, etc.).
     When Difference = $0.00, close the reconciliation.

  4. MONTH VERIFIED
     When every account for that month is reconciled and the books pass
     integrity checks, the month shows as closed on the home screen.
